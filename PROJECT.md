# VYANA VISION Website Project

## Project Status
Current version: v1.0  
Development branch: development  
Public branch: main  

## Project Goal
Create a premium, cinematic and responsive official website for the international music brand VYANA VISION.

The website should feel like a complete artistic universe where music, emotion and visual storytelling are connected.

## Core Brand Direction
- Premium
- Cinematic
- Futuristic
- Emotional
- Minimal
- International
- Responsive for Desktop and Mobile

## Main Visual Style
- Deep black background
- Neon purple and electric blue light
- Glowing V logo
- Futuristic night city
- Water reflections
- Studio microphone
- Female profile with headphones
- Glass-style interface elements
- Slow and elegant animations

## Approved Hero Concept
- Animated neon V logo
- Smooth intro sequence
- Live city lights
- Moving water reflections
- Weak reflected V in the water
- Responsive Desktop and Mobile layouts
- Glass buttons
- 30-second music previews
- Water reacts subtly while music is playing

## Main Navigation
- Home
- Music
- Videos
- The World of VYANA VISION
- News
- Collection
- Contact

## Planned Website Sections

### Home
Premium animated hero section.

### Music
Release cards, short previews, streaming links, lyrics and videos.

### Videos
Official music videos and cinematic previews.

### The World of VYANA VISION
Brand philosophy, creative process, stories, photos and behind-the-scenes material.

### News
New releases, premieres and project updates.

### Collection
Future online store for music, fashion, limited editions and digital products.

### Contact
Business, booking, media and collaboration enquiries.

## Development Principles
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Every change is tested before merging into main
- Main branch must always remain stable
- Effects must support music and emotion
- No visual effect should exist only for decoration
- Performance and loading speed are priorities
- Large media files should be optimized or hosted externally
