# VYANA VISION DESIGN SYSTEM

Version: 1.0  
Codename: Aurora

## Core Rule

We add only what makes VYANA VISION stronger.

Every element must strengthen at least one of:

- Music
- Emotion
- Brand

## Brand Experience

One Brand. One Atmosphere. One Universe.

The visual language of the website will later be adapted for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Spotify and other official platforms.

## Color Language

### Deep Black
`#050505`

Meaning: depth, silence, night and cinematic space.

### Electric Purple
`#8A2EFF`

Meaning: emotion, creativity and identity.

### Electric Blue
`#00C8FF`

Meaning: technology, clarity and modern sound.

### Glow Violet
`#B66CFF`

Meaning: atmosphere, dream and inspiration.

### Pure White
`#FFFFFF`

Meaning: voice, honesty and contrast.

### Soft Gray
`#C9C9C9`

Meaning: secondary information and calm readability.

## Typography

### Headings
Modern, elegant, geometric and internationally readable.

### Body Text
Clean, minimal and comfortable on Desktop and Mobile.

### Brand Name
Uppercase with increased letter spacing.

### Tagline
`MUSIC • EMOTION • VISION`

Thin weight with wide letter spacing.

## Logo V

- SVG format
- Sharp at every screen size
- Soft purple-blue neon glow
- Slow pulse
- Animated line drawing during the intro
- Short electric hover effect on Desktop
- Weak reflection in the water
- No aggressive flashing

## Hero Composition

### Desktop

- Studio microphone on the left
- Animated V logo in the central safe zone
- VYANA VISION below the logo
- Tagline below the brand name
- Glass buttons below the tagline
- Female profile with headphones on the right
- Futuristic city and water reflection at the bottom
- Important elements remain inside the central 60% of the screen

### Mobile

- Logo slightly smaller
- Compact buttons
- City and water remain visible below the logo
- Microphone and female profile remain recognizable
- No important content near the side edges
- Layout designed for narrow and tall screens
